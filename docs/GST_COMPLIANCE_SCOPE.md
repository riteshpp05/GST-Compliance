# GST Compliance Screening Engine Scope & Principles

## System Scope & Purpose
The **UC15 GST Compliance Intelligence & Resolution Agent** is designed exclusively as an **automated statutory screening and investigation assistance system** for enterprise tax and finance operations.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> UC15 does **not** act as a legal tax authority, nor does it render binding statutory legal conclusions. All findings generated by the system represent **screening indicators** that point out potential compliance anomalies requiring finance review.

---

## Standardized Outcome Classifications
Every transaction evaluated by the UC15 compliance screening engine produces one of the following standardized compliance status outcomes:

| Status Outcome | Definition & Financial Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`PASS`** | No issue detected by configured checks on the transaction date. |
| **`FAIL_CONFIGURED_CHECK`** / **`FAIL`** | Potential compliance issue identified by configured screening checks. |
| **`REVIEW_REQUIRED`** / **`NEEDS_REVIEW`** | Insufficient reference data certainty or ambiguous parameters requiring finance review. |
| **`INSUFFICIENT_DATA`** | Essential invoice or counterparty attributes missing to execute complete validation. |
| **`NOT_APPLICABLE`** | Specific statutory check does not apply to this transaction direction (e.g., ITC on outward AR sales). |

---

## Finance Review Indicators
Certain statutory conditions are explicitly modeled as **Review Indicators** rather than definitive legal disqualifications:
1. **Section 17(5) Keyword Matches:** Items containing keywords such as `food`, `gift`, `personal`, or `catering` emit a `Potential blocked-ITC indicator requiring finance review`.
2. **Missing GSTR-2B Entries:** Inward invoices absent from counterparty GSTR-2B filings emit a `Potential ITC reconciliation issue requiring finance review`.
3. **E-Way Bill Pending Status:** Shipments exceeding statutory threshold without confirmed EWB number emit a `Shipment compliance review indicator`.
